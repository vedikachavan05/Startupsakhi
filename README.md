# StartupSakhi – A Digital Business Platform for Women and Micro-Entrepreneurs

**StartupSakhi** is a web-based digital business platform designed to help women and micro-entrepreneurs transform their skills, talents, and home-based products or services into organized and manageable digital businesses.

Many aspiring entrepreneurs create products such as homemade food, handicrafts, flowers, clothing, gift hampers, and decorations, but may struggle with establishing an online presence, promoting their products, managing customer orders, and tracking business performance. Using multiple disconnected applications for these activities can make business management confusing and time-consuming.

StartupSakhi aims to bring these essential activities together in one simple, accessible, and mobile-friendly platform. Entrepreneurs can create business profiles, list their products or services, prepare promotional content using customizable templates, manage customers and orders, track payment status, and monitor business performance through a basic analytics dashboard.

The platform is designed with a user-friendly interface and multilingual support for English, Hindi, and Marathi, making it more accessible to users with different levels of digital literacy.

In the future, StartupSakhi can be enhanced with AI-based features such as demand prediction, personalized business recommendations, AI-assisted promotional content, and product suggestions based on available or leftover materials.

**The main objective of StartupSakhi is to simplify the journey from having a skill or home-based product to managing an organized digital business.**

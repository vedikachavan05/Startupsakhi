# 🌸 StartupSakhi
### Empowering Women Entrepreneurs. Connecting Local Businesses with Local Customers.

StartupSakhi is a community-focused digital marketplace designed to help women and girls running small, home-based and local businesses promote their products, reach potential customers and manage their orders through one simple platform.

Many small business owners depend on WhatsApp status updates, Instagram stories and personal contacts to find customers. StartupSakhi aims to bring these businesses together in one place, making local products easier to discover and helping women entrepreneurs grow their businesses digitally.

---

## 1. 🎯 Project Overview

StartupSakhi is a web-based marketplace that connects local women entrepreneurs (sellers) with customers.

The platform supports small businesses dealing in:

- Homemade food and bakery products
- Handicrafts and handmade products
- Flowers and gift items
- Tailoring and clothing
- Home décor and decoration services
- Other locally made products

**Our primary objective:** To provide women entrepreneurs with an accessible digital platform where they can showcase their products, attract customers and receive orders without needing advanced technical knowledge.

## 2. 💡 Problem Statement

Many women running small or home-based businesses face challenges such as:

- Limited customer reach.
- Dependence on social media and personal contacts.
- Difficulty promoting products consistently.
- Lack of a dedicated digital storefront.
- Difficulty keeping track of customer orders.
- Limited access to digital business opportunities.

StartupSakhi addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform for product discovery, business visibility and order management.

1. Seller creates an account.
2. Completes business registration.
3. Selects a business category.
4. Adds business and product details.
5. Uploads product photographs.
6. Publishes product listings.

2. Browses available businesses and products.
3. Views product details and seller information.
4. Selects a product and places an order.
5. Receives order status updates.
6. Coordinates delivery or pickup with the seller.

## 5. ⚖️ Product Quality, Responsibility and Seller Policies

StartupSakhi functions as a marketplace that connects independent sellers with customers. It does not manufacture, prepare, package or directly own the products listed by sellers.

### Seller Responsibility

Each seller is responsible for:

- Product quality and safety.
- Authenticity of product descriptions.
- Correct product photographs and specifications.
- Accurate pricing.
- Proper packaging and handling.
- Product preparation and hygiene, wherever applicable.
- Legal permissions, registrations and licences required for their business.
- Fulfilling accepted orders.
- Handling product-related complaints and applicable refunds or replacements.

### Product Quality Disclaimer

StartupSakhi does not independently manufacture, test, inspect, certify or guarantee the quality of every product listed by independent sellers.

The responsibility for ensuring that products meet applicable quality, safety, labelling and legal standards rests with the respective seller, subject to applicable law.

Product photographs and descriptions are provided by sellers. Customers should review the product information and seller details before placing an order.

**Important:** This marketplace policy does not remove any legal obligations StartupSakhi may have under applicable consumer-protection, food-safety or e-commerce laws. The platform will provide a complaint-reporting and grievance-handling mechanism and take appropriate action on reported violations.

### Seller Verification

The initial release may include:

- Basic seller identity and contact verification.
- Business profile review.
- Review of applicable registration and licence documents for regulated categories.
- Admin review of reported listings.
- Suspension or removal of listings that violate platform policies or applicable law.

Seller verification should not be represented as a guarantee of product quality.

## 6. 💰 GST and Business Compliance Policy

StartupSakhi aims to support small and emerging entrepreneurs while encouraging them to operate in accordance with applicable Indian laws.

GST registration is not automatically compulsory for every new seller. It depends on the nature of the business, aggregate turnover, location, supply type and applicable exceptions.

### A. GST Registration Thresholds

For general guidance under current GST rules:

| Business category | General threshold |
|---|---:|
| Exclusive supply of goods in eligible states | ₹40 lakh |
| Supply of services in most states | ₹20 lakh |
| Specified special-category states | Different, generally lower thresholds |

These are general thresholds, not universal exemptions. Certain businesses and transactions may require registration regardless of turnover.

### B. GST for Sellers on E-commerce Platforms

An important provision applies to small sellers supplying goods through e-commerce operators.

Under Notification No. 34/2023-Central Tax, certain unregistered sellers can supply goods through an eligible e-commerce operator without regular GST registration, subject to conditions including:

- Their aggregate turnover must remain within the applicable registration threshold.
- They must not make inter-state supplies of goods.
- They must supply through an e-commerce operator in only one State or Union Territory.
- They must have a valid PAN.
- They must declare the required details on the GST portal.
- They must obtain the required enrolment number before making such supplies through the platform.

This is a conditional exemption, not an automatic permission for every seller.

### C. GST Compliance Process at StartupSakhi

The proposed seller onboarding process will be:

1. Seller registers on StartupSakhi.
2. Seller selects their business category.
3. Seller declares whether they have GST registration.
4. GST-registered sellers can provide their GSTIN and applicable business details.
5. Eligible unregistered sellers may provide their GST enrolment details where the applicable e-commerce exemption is being used.
6. Sellers who are legally required to register must provide valid registration details before being allowed to make relevant supplies.
7. Sellers remain responsible for their own GST invoices, tax payments, returns and other applicable compliance.

### D. Platform-Level GST Responsibilities

StartupSakhi must also assess its own GST obligations.

If StartupSakhi operates as an e-commerce operator and collects consideration for taxable supplies made by registered third-party sellers, Section 52 of the CGST Act may require it to collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS), subject to applicable provisions and exemptions.

The currently notified general TCS rate is 0.5% of the applicable net taxable supplies where the statutory conditions apply.

This is a tax compliance mechanism, not an additional product-quality charge or a substitute for the seller's GST liability.

The exact applicability of GST registration, TCS, GSTR-8 and other platform obligations will be determined before commercial launch with a qualified GST professional, based on the final payment and marketplace model.

### E. GST Assistance for Sellers

Future platform improvements may include:

- GST registration guidance.
- Links to official GST registration resources.
- Information about applicable government schemes.
- Basic compliance reminders.
- Educational resources for new entrepreneurs.

**Official GST portal:** https://www.gst.gov.in/

## 7. 🍱 Food Product Policy

StartupSakhi may support homemade food and bakery businesses, subject to applicable food-safety requirements.

Sellers listing food products must:

- Obtain the applicable FSSAI registration or licence.
- Provide valid FSSAI details where required.
- Maintain food hygiene and safety.
- Provide accurate ingredient and product information.
- Follow applicable packaging, labelling and storage requirements.
- Ensure safe preparation, handling and delivery.

As a general FSSAI guideline, petty food businesses with annual turnover up to ₹12 lakh may qualify for basic registration, subject to the applicable eligibility criteria. Businesses exceeding the applicable limits or falling into other categories may require a State or Central licence.

StartupSakhi will not represent itself as an FSSAI-certified food inspection authority.

Food-category onboarding and the platform's own FSSAI obligations, if applicable, must be reviewed before commercial launch.

Official resources:
- FSSAI: https://www.fssai.gov.in/
- FoSCoS: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/
